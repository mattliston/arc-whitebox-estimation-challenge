# Carried content accounts for 50–79% of third-order kink-plane error

2026-10-01T21:22Z · finding · informational · measured

Monte Carlo truth of each mechanism that makes the post-activation third cumulant (gate-linear transport, Gaussian births, fourth-cumulant feed) on network 35 shows the card's kink-block plane error is 4.5% (layer 6) to 7.4% (layer 14) of the true plane; 50-79% of its square is carried distinct-index content and the fresh part is dominated by the fourth-cumulant feed into births (d_c/2) g_i g_j Q_ijcc, of which the card's isotropic version explains only 21-27%. Gaussian restarts show the non-isotropic content is not young: content made in the last layer reproduces 8-10% of it, the last four layers 50-65%, so it needs a carrier, not a formula.

Recorded measurement:
