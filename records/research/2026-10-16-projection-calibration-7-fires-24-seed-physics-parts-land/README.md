# Projection calibration of 7 fires: 24-seed physics parts land within +-0.04%, 8-seed-screen parts need a +0.75% haircut

2026-10-16T16:26:21Z · finding · informational · measured

Comparing the actual board change with the pre-registered projection for the 7 fires since 10-15 split cleanly by basis: projections built from 24-seed noise-free physics or public-based numbers were unbiased; a cold-priced bill change was overstated by 0.30% (the warm law); 8-seed-screen-based projections were overstated by 0.75% on average (winner s curse). Rule adopted: a fire built only from 24-seed-confirmed parts needs about 0.1% margin, any 8-seed-only part gets a +0.75% haircut, and bill parts must be priced warm.

Recorded measurement:

    Pooled bias +0.36, sd 0.41. (a) 24-seed/public basis: 336294 -0.571 -> -0.534, 336334 -0.162 -> -0.186, 336412 -0.25 -> -0.291: bias -0.01, spread +-0.04. (b) cold-priced bill: 336342 -0.707 -> -0.409 (+0.30). (c) 8-seed-screen: 336381 -0.38 -> +0.158, 336389 -0.56 -> +0.126, 336392 -0.75 -> +0.271: +0.54 / +0.69 / +1.02, mean +0.75. DCV2+L15RC30 warm cut -0.72% vs cold -0.51%.
