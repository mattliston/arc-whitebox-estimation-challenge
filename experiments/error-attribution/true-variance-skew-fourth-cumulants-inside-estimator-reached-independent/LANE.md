## 3. Coincidence-resummed one-step generator (`k3gen.py`) — design and certificates
k3'_i = Σ_jkl W_ji W_ki W_li κ3(x_j,x_k,x_l) split by index coincidence: [D] j=k=l exact univariate Edgeworth-4 post third cumulant; [P] j=k≠l: 3 Σ W_ji² W_li K21post_jl with K21post from the exact bivariate pair series in the OFF-diagonal bond (pairmap → the engine's emission_K21); [T] all distinct: Gaussian triple Hermite expansion Σ_{abc} ρ_jk^a ρ_kl^b ρ_jl^c c_j[a+c]c_k[a+b]c_l[b+c]/(a!b!c!) restricted to PATH words (one zero bond power) as Hadamard-power GEMMs on the off-diagonal correlation R (ρ^{∘q} = I + R^{∘q}), with the coincident j=l terms removed (they are plane-class); TRIANGLE words (a,b,c ≥ 1) dropped (1024⁴). Cost per layer at NB=6: 5 GEMMs for U^{(a)} = R^{∘a}(W∘c_a), 5 for the j=l corrections, plus the pair map (~1 GEMM-eq) ≈ 11 GEMM-eq ≈ 1.8% adj per layer if billed (0.161%/GEMM-eq).
Certificates: (1) triple series (all words, order ≤ 14) vs exact 3D Gauss-Hermite quadrature: rel 5e-4 / 4e-4 / 2e-5 at ρ ≈ 0.2 / 0.45 / 0.6; path-word share of the distinct κ3 at those correlations 0.97 / 0.92 / 0.89 (triangles are 3-11% of the distinct class for positively correlated triples). (2) GEMM path contraction vs the explicit distinct-triple sum (n=24): rel 4e-15. (3) univariate Gaussian k3post vs MC (4M): 0.24%.

## 8. cum4 → cum3 response: the (2,1,1) class as a card-side CP3 feed
Derivation (first-order multivariate Edgeworth, f = φ_aφ_bφ_c for distinct a,b,c): only κ4 entries whose index multiset lies in {a,b,c} respond; the engine's plane feed covers (3,1), (2,2), (4) via K31/K22/k4 and LACKS the (2,1,1) class κ4_aabc (a 1024³ object). Its response is ½ κ4_aabc E_G[φ''_a φ'_b φ'_c] (+ the b- and c-doubled terms); no sub-function (single or pair) contains {a,a,b,c}, so — unlike the κ3 plane feed whose t=0 part cancels against the disconnected subtraction (engine :516-520) — the separable leading kernel pdf_a/σ_a · Φ(α_b) · Φ(α_c) survives. With the card's pre-state CP4 κ4 = Σ_r λ_r u_r⊗u_r⊗u_r⊗u_r this is a CP3 tensor in the card's own (p,p,q) 3-permutation convention: λ'_r = ½ λ_r, p_r = u_r∘gate (Φ(α), the card's `gate`), q_r = u_r²∘birth_lambda (pdf/σ, the card's `birth_lambda`) — 2048 terms born per layer from state the card already holds (`_cp4_push` output + this layer's gate/birth_lambda). ## 10. Verdict (plain words)
1. The coincidence-resummed one-step generation adds nothing at 1024×16: diagonal and plane are already exact-series in the card, the born path words beyond the card's (1,1) births are 0.3% of k3, and a one-step map without the carried inherited content leaves 22-27% error even with exact inputs. The k3 deficit is in the CARRY.
2. The engine's carried k3u is worth ~half of the r35 ceiling (14.7 of 30 at β 0.33) and is over-scaled (β ≥ 0.8 negative); it stops at L13 while the card's error peaks at L14-15.
3. The derived cum4 (2,1,1) feed — 2048 CP3 terms per layer built from the card's own CP4 (λ'=½λ, p=u∘gate, q=u²∘birth_lambda) — is a real, transferable physics increment: k3 error at L8-14 falls ≈ 15% on both nets, GEMM-class (+21% util at full depth). On r35 it is the best k3 supply on record under the independent truth (16.3-16.5 points, above the engine's 14.7); on r100 the k3 channel itself converts negatively (truth k3: −1.7; the feed: −26), so the supply's k3-points are net-specific. The host mean gains from it only in deep windows (−2.6% r35 / −3.4% r100 for layers 7-15, +6.5% util) and every variant needs the F2J refit to show on the full card.
4. THE LEVER IS NOT UNIVERSAL: the same k3 deficit converts to +30 points on r35 and ≈ 0 on r100. Before any k3 supply is a bank candidate, the conversion must be understood on ≥ 4 nets under independent truth (draws: `mc_final_indep.py <rank> 4`, ~10 min CPU each).

## 11. CERTIFICATE of the cum4 → cum3 coefficient (cert_cum4_feed2.py / cert_cum4_feed2_sp.py): the (2,1,1) coefficient is ¼, not ½
Tiny world n=32: h = A z + m0 + s·He₃(z₁)·sv (κ3 ≡ 0 by symmetry; κ4 known ANALYTICALLY through all orders in s via joint cumulants of polynomials of one Gaussian — verified against a 40M-sample empirical κ4: ratios 0.88-1.09 on small entries, 1.00-1.02 on large ones).
