# Old positive-tail restoration: diagonal and radial channels interact strongly

2026-09-26 · finding · informational · measured

Omitting old-row reconstruction above standardized activation 2.5 at layers 7–14 and restoring its consumers separately showed that the diagonal channel accounts for most of the loss. Restoring both channels reproduced all 16 output layers exactly; these expensive diagnostic references do not establish deployable savings.

Recorded measurement:

    TRAIN35/100 raw change versus #332492: both diagonal and radial 0.0000%; neither +1.4463%; diagonal only +0.1707%; radial only +2.9972%; cheaper cutoff-2.5 implementation previously lost 1.8510% raw.
