# True (3,1) plane: norm-coupling law holds 12-16% of energy; mixed terms add 17-19 points

2026-10-02T15:56Z · finding · informational · measured

Against 8M truth planes of network 35 at layers 3-14, joint least squares explains the true (3,1) plane's energy as: the pair law 12 / 16 / 15 / 13 / 10%, plus the young pairs' fourth-chaos plane 19 / 23 / 21 / 17 / 14%, plus chains and older ages 1-2 points each, plus the mixed terms (first order in the carried third cumulant: a hub's second derivative, two legs on a bank column, one Gaussian edge) 30 / 42 / 41 / 36 / 32%, every fitted coefficient near 1. The remaining 55-65% is older fourth-chaos content carried through the gates. Energy is not value: the law's 15% earns 6-13% of error, the mixed terms' 18% earn 2-3%.
