# Rowwise (3,1) scaling and third-derived fourth cumulants lose most benefit with learned correction enabled

2026-09-23T02:20Z · finding · informational · measured

Stacking the per-row K31 row factor with a k3-derived per-unit k4 (beta .5) gives a large gain only when the learned closure is off; with it on, the K31 row alone keeps -1.52% while the k4 term turns positive, showing the learned parts already absorb most physics corrections and must be re-adapted for any physics change to pay.

Recorded measurement:

    learned ON: k31 -1.52% (.9872/.9834/.9991/.9695 on 35/100/37/39), k4 +0.66%, both -0.34%; learned OFF: k31 -3.41%, k4 -3.94%, both -7.2% (4/4, -5..-10%); k4 from k3 beta .3/.5/.7 at L8 (offdiag 0): r35 1.6507/1.6662/1.6919e-8, r100 1.6749/1.6853/1.7157e-8
