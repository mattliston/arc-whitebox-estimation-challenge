## Fixed30 FP32 feature slots

All slots are evaluated on the unmodified autonomous trajectory before its
physical update. n=1024, l=0..15, s=sigma, v=var, a=mu/s, g=Phi, p=phi,
h=pk1v. Let z3=D3/(v*s), z4=g4row/v²; unavailable first-layer cumulants are
zero. R=Coff/(s_i*s_j), T=D21/(v_i*s_j), both with the ORIGINAL zero diagonals.
These are new feature semantics, not a claim of byte/column compatibility
with COMP's30-feature provider or old normalizers.

| Slot | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
|0,1,2|2*l/15-1; l/16; constant1|
|3,4,5,6|a; log(s)-mean(log(s)); g; p|
|7,8,9|p+a*g; h/s; h/s-(p+a*g)|
|10,11,12|z3; z4; abs(a)|
|13,14,15|row mean(R); sqrt(row mean(R²)); row mean(abs(R))|
|16|sum_i W_ij /sqrt(2)|
|17|sum_i(W_ij²-2/n) *sqrt(n/8)|
|18|sum_i W_ij³ * n/sqrt(120)|
|19|sum_i(W_ij⁴-12/n²) *sqrt(n³/1536)|
|20,21|sqrt(row sum(T²)); sqrt(column sum(T²))|
|22,23|row sum(T)/sqrt(n); column sum(T)/sqrt(n)|
|24|(R*a)/n|
|25,26|row mean(abs(T)); column mean(abs(T))|
|27,28|log1p(abs(z3)); log1p(abs(z4))|
|29|constant across units equal to mean(h/s)|

W means original input-row/output-column w32, NOT its already-transposed
local alias. The explicit weight constants are the same analytical He-weight
standardizations, not trained coefficients. No clipping/centering of physical
state is added; feature denominators use the existing backbone v/s.

Final layer15 has no Coff/D21. Slots13,14,15,20,21,22,23,24,25,26 are EXACT
zero. Do not restore terminal covariance, D21, pair terms or postcovariance
summaries for features. D3 and g4 remain the actual carried scalar reads.
At layer0 the absent D21 and scalar cumulants are zero, while Coff is live.
No final feature depends on targets, a teacher-forced mean or correction.
