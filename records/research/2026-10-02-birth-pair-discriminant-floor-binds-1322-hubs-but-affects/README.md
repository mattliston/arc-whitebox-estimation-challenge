# Birth-pair discriminant floor binds on 13–22% of hubs but affects little innovation

2026-10-02T07:11Z · finding · informational · measured

The floor of the birth pair's discriminant (disc = 4 innovation / birth - 3 u^2, floored at 0.01 v^2) binds on 13-22% of the birth hubs at layers 6-14 (no real pair matches the hub's own third cumulant), but those are the nearly always-on units (gate 0.92-0.98) and what the floored pairs leave unmatched is at most 1.3% of the summed |innovation| and 0.1% of its energy. No lever.
