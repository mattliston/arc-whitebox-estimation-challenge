# Second-order Edgeworth marginal correction worsens raw error 2.5% at full strength

2026-09-23T00:30Z · experiment · rejected · measured

Adding the second-order marginal Edgeworth terms (the k3^2 He6/72 contributions to m, r2, r3, r4) worsens the final MSE on both research networks because the learned closure already compensates for the missing term; a refit would be needed and could release at most 1-2%.
