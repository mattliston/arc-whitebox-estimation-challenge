# Lower early roster floors increased capacity but bought only 0.5–0.6% error reduction per 1% bill

2026-10-17T20:56:25Z · experiment · rejected · measured

Lowering early live, response and birth roster floors initially looked favorable on two networks but failed on the full seven-network judge. Lowering only live and response floors or relaxing the early dead cut also failed to yield an adjusted gain without a refit.

Recorded measurement:

    Birth floor .005 at layers 1–6: raw -0.75% (SE .39), bill +1.44%, adjusted +0.68%; all three: raw -1.20% (SE .68), bill +2.12%, adjusted +0.89%; live plus response: raw -0.50%, bill +0.99%; dead cut 4.0: raw -0.07%, bill +0.06%.
