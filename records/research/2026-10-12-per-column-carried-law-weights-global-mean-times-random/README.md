# Per-column carried-law weights are a global mean times random deviations of about 4%

2026-10-12T23:35Z · finding · rejected · measured

The proposed per-column replacement of the law's global transport factor 2 gbar (2 sum_i gam_i pp_ir^2 / sum pp_ir^2) was closed without a run: the fourth-cumulant sum N_ab = sum_k kappa4(y_a, y_b, y_k, y_k) transports with the mean of gam over the summed unit k, which W spreads over the next layer's units with iid weights, so per-column refinements are random at the +-4% level; the proposal weighted the wrong index.
