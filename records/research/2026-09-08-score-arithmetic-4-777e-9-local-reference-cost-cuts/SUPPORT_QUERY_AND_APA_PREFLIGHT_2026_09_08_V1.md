## 2. A conditional distinct-only repair, not a score improvement

For a deficit r=-s_i(w)>0, set x=w-w_i*e_i; if needed restrict x to the
physical active support as well. Let G=||x||^4-sum_a x_a^4>0. The
minimum-Frobenius single-halfspace repair within all-distinct tensors is

    DeltaT[z^3] = (3*r/G)*z_i*((x.T*z)^2-sum_a x_a^2*z_a^2).

If x is additionally restricted to active support, the minimization is
within that active-supported all-distinct subspace, not the unrestricted
all-distinct space.

Root independently checked that the unscaled tensor has norm squared
G/3 and its (e_i,w,w) query equals G/3. Thus the repair gives exactly
DeltaT(e_i,w,w)=r, preserves every current repeated entry, and gives
DeltaT(w,w,w)=3*w_i*r on that same ray. Its direct Gaussian/cubic mean
readout is -alpha*phi(alpha)*w_i*r/(2*v). This excludes head response,
covariance, radial changes, other queries and all descendants.

Three cubes realize the first term:

    r/(2*G)*[((e_i+x).z)^3+((e_i-x).z)^3-2*z_i^3],

where the dot denotes an inner product. The coordinate-star subtraction
is -(3*r/G)*z_i*sum_a x_a^2*z_a^2. This is a real-arithmetic identity;
FP32 repeated-plane cancellation still requires qualification.

The projection moves toward a feasible true T only conditional on correct
fixed m,C and repeated entries. Those current owners are approximate.
It does not identify which owner is wrong or imply improved scored means.
Multiple constraints can interfere; no selection, iteration or strength
protocol is authorized by this identity.

Source inspection found two nontrivial layout traps. Appending three

## 4. Approximate multiplication: finite arithmetic and precision

Primary sources provide real Bini <2,2,3> rank10 and Schonhage <3,3,3>
rank21 approximate circuits; the authors' coefficient repository also
contains a Smirnov rank20 approximate circuit. These are not the already
closed exact rank40 implementation.

- https://arxiv.org/html/1409.2908, sections1.1 and2.2.3.
- https://github.com/arbenson/fast-matmul/tree/master/codegen/algorithms
- https://membres-ljk.imag.fr/Jean-Guillaume.Dumas/Publications/p14_BoDu.pdf

4.7619% /11.1111% /15.3439% for rank10/21/20 respectively. These omit
divisibility, transforms, precision, assembly and all other card work.

Bini's truncation is O(epsilon) and rounding can be amplified as

No prototype is admitted without one specified, cancellation-controlled
