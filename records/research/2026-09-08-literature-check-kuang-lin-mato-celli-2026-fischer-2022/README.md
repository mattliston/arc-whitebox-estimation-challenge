# Literature check: Kuang-Lin, Mato, Celli 2026 and Fischer 2022 give no fixed-weight ReLU joint-state constructor

2026-09-08T09:01Z · decision · rejected · conjectured

Four public sources were read for a usable estimator: the Kuang and Lin v2 Gaussian moment-matching revision (exact Gaussian maps retain no non-Gaussian joint state), Mato et al. (equilibrium recurrent networks with regular odd activations and weight-averaged correlators), Celli's 2026 Edgeworth bounds (conditioned on random weights with fixed inputs) and Fischer et al. 2022 (fixed weights, Gram-Charlier ReLU responses, the familiar moment expansion). None supplies an affordable current joint-state constructor; the record calls this a source-scope decision, not an empirical rejection of a translated algorithm.
