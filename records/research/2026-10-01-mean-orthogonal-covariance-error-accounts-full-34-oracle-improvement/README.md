# Mean-orthogonal covariance error accounts for the full 34% oracle improvement

2026-10-01T19:35Z · finding · informational · measured

The card's carried covariance error E on network 35 (layers 6, 10, 13, 32M truth) was split with the unit mean direction mh into alpha mh mh', the cross term (mh d' + d mh') and the orthogonal part Eperp. In closed loop (truth removed at layers 2-14, learned correction on, no refit) removing the alpha term or the cross term changes the final error by 0 to +1.6%, while Eperp alone gives -33 to -34% and all of E -29 to -30%. The whole covariance lever is the part orthogonal to the mean; the same law held on the September card.
