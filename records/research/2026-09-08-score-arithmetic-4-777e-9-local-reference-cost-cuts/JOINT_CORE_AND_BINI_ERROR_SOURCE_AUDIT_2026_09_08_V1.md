## 2. Cycle cores and leading4-to3 emissions: exact model algebra

For a CHOSEN symmetric PSD source approximation C=L L.T, let ell_i be
row i of L and A_i=d_i ell_i ell_i.T. For quadratic Gaussian coordinates
y_i=d_i*(Z_i^2-C_ii)/2, their cumulant generating function gives

    kappa_r[y(z)] = (r-1)!/2 * trace(A(z)^r),
    A(z)=sum_i z_i A_i,  r>=2.

Thus the pure four-quadratic cycle is Q[z^4]=3 trace(A(z)^4).
For symmetric matrix arguments its polarization is

    Q(A,B,C,D)=trace(ABCD+ABDC+ACBD),
    Q31_ij=3 trace(A_i^3 A_j),
    Q22_ij=2 trace(A_i^2 A_j^2)+trace(A_i A_j A_i A_j).

These include repeated entries for the chosen source; replacing the real
covariance by L L.T remains an approximation with its own missing value.
The exchange term can be computed from chunked products R_ij=A_i A_j,
then sum_ab R_ij,ab R_ij,ba. This is O(n^2 k^3), with chunked
O(b n k^2) workspace. A k^4-feature Gram is therefore NOT mandatory.
Numerous small products, FNP billing, dispatch and actual memory remain
unqualified; big-O is not a runtime or complete-cost certificate.

One cohort can also share its LEADING4-to3 descendants. Use an orthonormal
symmetric-matrix vectorization U_i=svec(A_i), h=k(k+1)/2, and symmetric
h-by-h V_i. Define

    T_ijk = U_j.T V_i U_k + U_i.T V_j U_k + U_i.T V_k U_j.

Both U and V push linearly along the neuron coordinate. With incoming
cores A_i and Gaussian gate/curvature g_i,d_i, set

    U_i' = g_i U_i,
    V_i' = g_i V_i + .5*d_i H(A_i),
    H(A): X -> A^2 X + X A^2 + A X A.

H(A) is restricted to Sym_k; its h-by-h coefficients can be assembled in
O(k^4) after A^2, rather than h^2 separate k^3 trace evaluations.
For a pure fourth-source sidecar V initially vanishes. Treating these
cores as an entire quadratic probability-law replacement would silently
add their lower cumulants and is a DIFFERENT unqualified proposal.

The post repeated-third plane can reuse the already-read incoming planes:

    D'_ij = g_i^2*g_j D_ij
             +d_i*g_i*g_j Q31_ij +.5*d_j*g_i^2 Q22_ij.

general post-plane construction solely for repeated restoration. It is
real-arithmetic equality for the declared leading-response model, not
stored-FP32 parity or an exact full-ReLU distributional update.

        [(b-f)i-(d+e)g+(c+f)j, (a+d)g+(e-a)h-(b+c)j],
