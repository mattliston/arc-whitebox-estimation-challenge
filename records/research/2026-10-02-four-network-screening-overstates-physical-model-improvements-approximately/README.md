# Four-network screening overstates physical-model improvements by approximately twofold

2026-10-02T03:17Z · lesson · informational · measured

Levers screened on networks 35, 100, 37 and 39 (the networks the parent's kernels and parameters were selected on) overstated their held-out value: K4L alone read -2.83% there and about -1.4% on 24 held-out ranks, F4b read -4.36% against F4's -2.33% there but was no better than F4 on 24 held-out ranks, and a dead-row threshold change that read 1% on networks 35 and 100 was break-even on fresh ranks. Rule: every lever is judged on held-out newer ranks (at least six for a dial) or on generated networks nobody tuned on.

Recorded measurement:

    K4L screen -2.83% vs held-out -1.41%; F4b screen -4.36% vs held-out -3.36% (F4 -3.54%); dead-row threshold 3.0: networks 35 / 100 MSE -2.0% / -0.7% vs fresh ranks break-even; birth dials: first four ranks -0.4% reversed by ranks 287 and 641
