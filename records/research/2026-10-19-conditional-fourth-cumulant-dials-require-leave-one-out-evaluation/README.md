# Conditional fourth-cumulant dials require leave-one-out evaluation; in-sample estimates overstate gain threefold

2026-10-19T20:10:00Z · lesson · informational · measured

A conditional dial fitted on the store ranks must be judged with per-rank leave-one-out coefficients (the k4cvloo pattern, rank parsed from the network name), because the in-sample read overstated the honest one three times (-0.48% vs -0.17%); the k4 band, ages 3-4 and pair-law strength dials sit at their network-average optimum and are not to be re-run as value arms. A conditional k4 dial needs a genuinely predictive truth-free feature (oracle ceiling about -0.5% band 2, -0.3% band 3), not a linear fit on 28 networks.
