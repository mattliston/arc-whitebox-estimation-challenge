# Seven-level odd-64 strips trade 0.70% lower charged work for approximately 12.8 seconds of wall time

2026-10-02T08:29Z · experiment · rejected · measured

22% of the bill is packet pushes and 30% plane reads that run at 6 recursion levels because a dimension is not a multiple of 128; moved to 7 levels with half-zero strips and pads they save 2-5% each, not the 7-9% of a clean multiple. Every paying product: bill -0.70% for about +12.8 s of wall; products saving more than 3%: -0.43% for +4.3 s and about +1,000 requests; the best single class (layer-6 push, inner dimension 960) -0.195% for +1.6 s. With no wall room under the designation rule and 10 s per 1% the trade is dearer than the 7-level dials already on (6 s per 1%). Priced on a fitted bill model, not built.

Code availability: Only the cost-estimation program is retained for the seven-level strip proposal. The native seven-level implementation was not built.
