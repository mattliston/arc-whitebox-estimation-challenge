# Qualification of the source-screen operation counts

Additive note; preserve RAW_CONSTRUCTION_SOURCE_SCREEN_2026_09_07_V1.md.
Its4Kn³ conditional-covariance work and2n²R contraction counts describe the
leading arithmetic of conventional dense multiply/add implementations.
They are not exact FlopScope bills or lower bounds against Winograd/other
fast circuits. In particular, read "at least4Kn³" in that source note only
as the listed conventional circuit's affine skeleton, not an algorithmic
lower bound. Additional nonlinear/feedback/initialization work remains
unpriced without an explicit implementation. The no-run decision rests on
the missing new information and complete affordable construction, not on
a proved universal cost obstruction. No numerical result changes.
