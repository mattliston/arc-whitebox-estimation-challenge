# Per-row plane gain explains 20% of third-order error at layer 12 and depends on scale and kurtosis

2026-10-02T20:00Z · finding · informational · measured

A perfect row gain fitted to one truth draw and scored on another removes 20% of the estimated third-order plane error at layer 12. Log standard deviation, local kurtosis and the plane's mean-direction row sum jointly explain 0.50-0.61 of the wanted gain variance at late layers, while normalized mean, gate and density do not.

Recorded measurement:

    error removed at layers2/5/9/12: 0/7/15/20%; wanted gain mean +0.5% layers3-5, +1.3% layer9, +1.5 to+3.9% layers12-14; rms1.1% layer3, 3% layer9, 4-5% layer12, 6% layer14; late joint R^2 0.50-0.61; log-sigma slope -(0.068+0.033(l-3)), kurtosis +4 to+6, plane-mean coefficient -7 to-27
