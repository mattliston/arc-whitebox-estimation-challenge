# Layer-12 plane error is distributed across ages: per-age fitting removes 11.3%, young ages only 0.3%

2026-10-11T18:35:30Z · finding · informational · measured

Re-measured at S47e (network 35, layer 12, third-cumulant plane with index multiplicities (2,1) (K21) as the covariance uses it, truth = 8M-sample planes, ages from Gaussian-restart planes): the moment-closure estimator holds 99.68% of the truth plane's energy (error 0.33%); by age of content the moment-closure estimator's coefficient falls smoothly from 0.989 / 0.988 / 0.985 (ages 1-3, the young cohorts) to 0.983 ... 0.977 (ages 4-11, the old tier after its joins), the same profile as Oct 2. A joint per-age fit of the error removes only 11.3% of its energy, of which the young ages hold 0.3%; about 89% is content inside each age that the moment-closure estimator misplaces, so the K21 error is not held by any carrier and carrier-scale searches were stopped.
