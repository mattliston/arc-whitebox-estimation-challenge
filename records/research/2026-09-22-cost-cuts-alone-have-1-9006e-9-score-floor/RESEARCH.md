**Research assessment for the approximately 1e-9 adjusted-score goal — 22 September 2026.**

The strongest research direction is to improve the *coupled moment state and its final-output consumer*, while developing a cheaper way to represent the useful information. Neither a cheaper copy of the present predictor nor another small terminal feature is, by itself, supported as a route to 1e-9. This note records the evidence, quantitative requirements, hypotheses, and tests worth considering. It does not claim that a successful new algorithm has been found.

The user requested this assessment while the fixed incoming-moment readout fit runs. That fit, its schedule, and its queued native/downstream/release gates are unchanged. No additional numerical worker, target-array access, candidate, or upload was started for this assessment. Proposed tests below are future research designs, not experimental results. Source bindings and reproducible arithmetic accompany this note in `source_review.json` and `economics.json`.

**The current bank and the size of the gap.** Submission [331824](https://www.aicrowd.com/challenges/arc-white-box-estimation-challenge-2026/submissions/331824) completed all 100 networks with no failures: adjusted score 3.5070948899311718e-9 and raw mean squared error 1.9005926183979227e-8. Its exact 368,577-byte approved archive has already been submitted; its SHA-256 is 93f075402ae30c2a7996d53176081fb080ef6be8ab3f626516759b7f755c2496. The local authoritative record is [official_best_331824.json](../official_best_331824.json). The 4e-9 milestone is banked; 3e-9, 2e-9, and approximately 1e-9 remain unmet. Standing approval covers future qualified improvements.

For network i, let E_i be final-layer MSE and F_i its complete charged work. Phase 2 scores the average of E_i max(0.1, F_i / B), with B = 2^41 = 2,199,023,255,552. The floor begins at 219.902 billion operations. The actual aggregate is **not** generally mean(E) times mean(F)/B. The current round specifies width 1024, depth 16, 120 seconds for prediction, 0.4 seconds residual time, and 8 GB memory. These are official documentation facts, separate from our stricter local guards. [Round specification](https://github.com/AIcrowd/whest-starterkit/blob/main/docs/reference/rounds.md), [scoring model](https://github.com/AIcrowd/whest-starterkit/blob/main/docs/concepts/scoring-model.md).

The bank spends 381.025–425.364 billion operations per network, mean 402.386 billion. Its observed worst prediction wall time is 110.168 seconds and worst residual time 0.385388 seconds. The FLOP budget has room, but the observed wall-time and residual margins are small; a promising mathematical method can still be undeployable.

Three arithmetic conclusions sharply constrain the search:

- **Cost reduction alone bottoms out at 1.9005926184e-9** if predictions stay identical. Even at the scoring floor, approximately 1e-9 needs at least a **47.38% raw-error reduction**. At unchanged per-network costs, a uniform reduction of every network's error needs **71.49%**.
- A uniform **14.459% cost reduction**, with predictions unchanged, reaches 3e-9. Every current bill remains above the floor in that scenario, so the scaling is exact. The floor first binds at a cost factor 0.577134 and binds every network at 0.516974. Extrapolating the linear score formula through that interval is incorrect.
- As a purely illustrative combined target, cutting every error by 53% and every cost by 40% would give about **0.9890e-9**; all scaled current costs would still be above the floor. Neither change is demonstrated. This shows why a substantial accuracy improvement plus a substantial efficiency improvement is a plausible *shape* of solution.

Above the floor, adding dF to one network needs fractional MSE improvement greater than dF/(F+dF) merely to break even. At the current mean bill, the running readout's approximately 9.404 billion extra operations require about **2.28%** raw improvement. Adding 45 or 100 billion requires approximately **10.06%** or **19.91%**, respectively. Each actual experiment must instead use its own per-network bills.

**What the accumulated evidence says.** The best physical clue is the old Lazy mean-source decomposition. On rank35, with independent reference streams, formal removal of the third- and fourth-moment mismatch terms jointly gave error ratio 0.665870; rank100's single-stream version gave 0.818586. Marginal closure residual had almost no signed endpoint alignment on rank35. These are privileged tangent calculations on an older estimator, not deployable interventions, generalization results, or gains that can be multiplied into the current official score. They nevertheless favor better joint moment information over another isolated marginal Edgeworth term. [Source decomposition](../lazy_mean_source_resolution_v1/RESULT.md).

The equally important counterexample is the old incoming joint-moment head. It improved several local moment-plane metrics, but its frozen native deployment against 331760 made pooled raw error **1.495641 times worse**, and adjusted error **1.530216 times worse**. The current fit is deliberately different: it trains only 96 zero-start output weights on final error while preserving the existing physical features and bank. A better local moment approximation can interact badly with a consumer that has learned to compensate for the old approximation. Transfer between parents, autonomous feedback, and local-objective mismatch are plausible explanations; that old experiment did not isolate which explanation is correct. [Native result](../current_p_joint_moment_native_v1/RESULT.md), [current fit protocol](../current_p_incoming_moment_readout_fit_v1/PROTOCOL.md).

There is also strong evidence against treating representation energy as the right compression objective. Plain rank256 saved about 12% of cost but raised raw error 33–36%, worsening adjusted error about 18.7% on its two old development networks. An exact Gram retained 99.6567% of one-mode energy yet badly damaged relevant tensor queries. A Gaussian local-mean-sensitivity basis also failed the native endpoint. These results concern specific representations and old parents; they do not prove that every compact state must fail. [Rank256 endpoint](../rank256_objective_v1/NEGATIVE_RESULT.md), [exact Gram](../full_tensor_exact_gram_information_v1/NEGATIVE_RESULT.md), [consumer-weighted basis](../consumer_omega384_v1/NEGATIVE_RESULT.md).

**First lever: co-adapt the supplied moments and the predictor that consumes them.** This has the clearest immediate decision path because the running fit already isolates a small part of it. If the 96-weight readout passes native and downstream gates, bank the improvement after full release qualification, then use the new frozen bank for further comparisons. Its measured gain will tell us whether the existing physical features contain information that the old final predictor was failing to use. It will not establish that all missing moment information is now available.

If it improves raw error but loses after its 9.404-billion-operation charge, the next useful idea is a *cheaper causal representation of the successful correction*. If it worsens native raw error, distinguish lack of useful information from the limitations of the approximate training arithmetic before investing in a larger jointly trainable model. The terminal result remains the only eligible checkpoint; neither intermediate training weights nor validation-selected amplitudes become candidates.

A future co-adaptation study would need a real change in mechanism: for example, simultaneously updating a small physical moment branch and the part of P that consumes it, with final-layer loss and a fixed bank-preserving initialization. It should compare the coupled model with the same frozen-consumer control on a prespecified network split. It should require complete native adjusted improvement, not just lower local moment error. This is an experiment to consider only after the present result, not a recommendation to blindly unfreeze everything: an older joint P/covariance fit already lost by a factor about 1.77, and a six-epoch P/Q fit barely changed validation error and failed its gates. More epochs or more parameters alone have no demonstrated large upside. [Earlier fit review](../research_sources_checkpoint153.json).

**Second lever: represent the missing joint remainder, rather than add one more scalar cumulant term.** This is the higher-upside but higher-risk scientific direction. A useful state may need compatible directed K21 and K31 planes, symmetric K22, marginal third/fourth cumulants, and the descendants emitted when nonlinear gates act on them. Improving one of these while implicitly leaving contradictory values in the others can spoil the final prediction. A small family of gate-conditioned response statistics is another possible representation, but it must supply new causal information; renaming the existing W-transformed hidden features does not do so.

The main trap is to assume that a quadratic Gaussian latent model plus a full Gaussian residual covariance closes the problem. That route has already been derived and partially tested. In the archived counterexample, H2(z) + epsilon H4(z) and H2(z) + eta, with independent Gaussian eta of variance 24 epsilon squared, have the same mean and variance but different third moments. The former has third moment 8 + 72 epsilon + 576 epsilon squared + 1728 epsilon cubed; the latter has 8. The mixed remainder law matters even when its covariance is exact. Earlier causal quadratic constructions and a four-component Gaussian-mixture propagation were also uncompetitive. [Quadratic route audit](../../astra_phase2_reexamination_v1/CONNECTED_GATE_SUBTRACTION_AND_QUADRATIC_ROUTE_AUDIT_V1.md), [mixture result](../../astra_phase2_reexamination_v1/POSITIVE_GMM4_SPLIT_MERGE_V1_CLOSED.md).

The cheapest useful next test is therefore a **constructive small-width closure test**, not another full-width fit. Specify exactly what state distinguishes such mixed-law counterexamples; show how it is acquired from the current layer, transported, and reset after ReLU; verify its mean/covariance/K21/K31/K22 consistency on a fixed two-layer example against a separately computed reference. Then count every acquisition, push, reset, and read operation before considering a 1024-wide experiment. A state that cannot distinguish the counterexample, needs unavailable oracle moments, or omits its nonlinear reset is not ready for a full-network trial.

Cost is a serious constraint. The archived r=32 quadratic-core subtotal is about 8.995 billion conventional operations per layer *before* a complete nonlinear reset. A generic alternating fourth-order trace term alone can add 68.719 billion per layer. Those are construction-specific subtotals, not lower bounds or native bills. The desired new mechanism is a cheap structured approximation to the *consumed mixed remainder*, with measured endpoint value; storing a nominally complete fourth-order tensor is not a practical plan. Earlier fourth/odd-lineage and T-times-C constructions show how quickly modest information can become too expensive. [Fourth lineage](../shared_fourth_odd_lineage_v1/NEGATIVE_RESULT.md), [terminal T-times-C information](../terminal_tc_information_v1/RESULT.md).

**Third lever: distill useful expensive information into a genuinely cheaper causal estimator.** This offers a way to combine accuracy and efficiency instead of paying for every new feature at deployment. The teacher must first be demonstrably useful; an expensive oracle fitted separately to each network's answers is not an admissible deployable teacher. Training can use additional derived information, but the deployed student must compute all of its own inputs within the charged execution.

A concrete discriminating target is the difference between a richer predictor and a compact predictor on the *same TRAIN networks*. That difference is free of Monte Carlo label noise and isolates the information lost by the compact representation. Train a small residual predictor using only states that the compact runtime really possesses, then assess both teacher agreement and true final error on a fixed network split. Include a truth-only compact control: good teacher matching can otherwise hide inherited teacher bias. An even stronger future teacher could combine a validated new moment source with the bank; there is no such new validated teacher today.

The old rank256 diagnostic provides a useful scale, not permission to rescue that closed experiment. Its final prediction-change MSE was about 6.1–6.4e-9. Under the explicitly optimistic approximation that residual compression error is orthogonal to bank error, retaining the old roughly 12% saving would require removing about 60% of that added error merely to break even. Complete recovery of the current bank's predictions at 88% of its cost would still score about **3.086e-9**, short of 3e-9. A new student architecture therefore needs additional cost saving or a small genuine accuracy gain even for the next milestone. It needs much more for 1e-9.

The key novelty would be a cheap predictor of *endpoint-relevant missing information*, learned across networks, rather than selecting a tensor subspace by Frobenius energy or a local Gaussian sensitivity. If a fixed small information test cannot recover enough of the lost endpoint signal, stop before a large fit. Charge its feature acquisition and hidden-state propagation; a 96-parameter output can still require billions of operations to produce its inputs. Existing terminal affine/intermediate features already failed several cross-network tests, so the required causal inputs cannot simply be assumed to exist. [Terminal representation result](../terminal_representation_v1/NEGATIVE_RESULT.md).

**Fourth lever: improve agreement between training and the actual native objective, where there is evidence that it matters.** The present CPU gradients are approximate proposals. Exact Adam/RNG transport proves faithful continuation of those proposals, not the derivative of the full native predictor. Earlier component VJPs passed, but a complete forward comparison still missed its 1e-5 bound; an earlier native d50 search and a 16-coefficient SPSA search also failed their scientific gates. Therefore “use native gradients” is an unsolved engineering task, not a ready improvement. [Gradient-history audit](../current_p_native_gradient_history_audit_v1/RESULT.md).

A bounded future diagnostic could fix a small set of parameter directions and perturbation sizes on TRAIN before any scores are inspected, and compare CPU-predicted changes with actual cold-native loss changes. Its purpose would be to determine whether disagreement is large enough to reverse useful updates in the new branch. It must report inconclusive finite differences when numerical variation or curvature dominates. It should not become an open-ended search over favorable directions, amplitudes, or validation networks. Only a demonstrated systematic discrepancy warrants the cost of a more faithful trainer.

The eventual training objective can also weight networks by their actual scoring multiplier. Current bills vary by only a factor about 1.116, so this is a sensible alignment detail with no demonstrated factor-of-three benefit. The live fit remains unchanged. Repeated training on the same data is a lower priority than adding information or verifying a concrete arithmetic mismatch; the older six-epoch result and reference-noise audit do not support undertraining or noisy labels as the established main bottleneck. The latter was a model-based two-network audit, not a measured universal noise floor. [Noise audit](../../astra_phase2_reexamination_v1/TEACHER_REFERENCE_NOISE_AUDIT_V1_CLOSED.md).

**Fifth lever: pursue cheap bankable gains, with a separate standard for high-upside research.** The history contains small positive signals that failed deliberately large research-opportunity thresholds. For example, the shared-pair fourth feature improved its old cross-network control by 0.443% at roughly 0.07% feature overhead, but failed its original 3%/5% magnitude gates. That result remains closed and is not a validated current-bank improvement. It does demonstrate why “failed opportunity gate” must not be recorded as “zero information.” [Small fourth-feature result](../shared_pair_fourth_feature_v1/RESULT.md).

Future genuinely distinct, cheap designs should use prospectively declared improvement gates consistent with the user's instruction to bank improvements, alongside the existing generalization, resource, and complete-cost requirements. They should not need a 20% effect to deserve banking. Conversely, a small effect should not be advertised as solving the 1e-9 problem. The terminal cubic-query oracle offered only 10–12% extra raw improvement beyond its affine oracle, with about 14.4 billion added operations and no shared cross-network predictor; it is a conditional feature lead, not an available 18% competition gain. Correlated weak features cannot have their improvements added together. [Cubic-query result](../terminal_cubic_hidden_queries_v1/RESULT.md).

One promising form for a *new* inexpensive branch would be a factorized or shared computation of an already useful correction, with the same physical meaning and fewer materialized pairwise nonlinear features. First derive the algebra and complete bill; then test the fixed construction against a common control. A generic larger terminal MLP, another scalar rescaling of a failed output, or a collection of validation-selected weak features does not meet that description.

**Efficiency work needs an actual uncovered cost center.** Numeric transformed-weight reuse is already in the current bank. `_W5_PortCache` stores real FP32 leaves, not merely array views, and explicitly serves covariance, shared-basis, newborn, and star-push roles. A full 1024-by-1024 eligible cache hit removes 37,710,848 operations; its creator copy is separately charged. Recommending this reuse as new would repeat completed work. The recent structural-zero/recursive-fringe search added only about 0.02% beyond its existing family and failed its 3% opportunity threshold. These are scoped negatives, not a proof that no faster circuit exists. [Current candidate](../current_p_message_covariance_release_v1/candidate.py), [structural-zero result](../structural_zero_cost_frontier_v1/RESULT.md).

Before another kernel project, identify a substantial *paid* operation absent from existing reuse, establish whether its numerical association can remain acceptable, and count copies, reconstruction, and additional storage. For a cost-only route to 3e-9 the aggregate target is roughly 14.46%, well beyond a small transform saving. Output-aware compression or sharing whole expensive state computations has more plausible magnitude than another minor leaf-level reduction, but neither is validated.

Network/layer-adaptive compute is conditional on having useful cheaper and richer variants. A router must be learned from TRAIN comparisons and use only causal runtime features; it cannot run several full estimators for free and then pick the best. Merely equalizing current bills at their mean, with unchanged predictions, would improve the aggregate by only about **0.836%**, as derived from the observed error/cost covariance. This narrow identity does not bound a new router that changes both error and cost, but it rules out treating the present cost variation itself as a large free opportunity.

**Ideas to keep low on the list.** Widening the shared sketch to FP64 already added about 11% adjusted cost without a useful endpoint accuracy gain. The negative covariance modes were tiny under their tested criteria; clipping them is not a demonstrated large fix. Marginal closure refinement has weak endpoint alignment in the old source decomposition. A first-layer quadratic randomized-QMC control was already far from competitive on the three completed networks and failed fidelity on the fourth; it was not a successful four-network experiment. These conclusions retain their original scopes. [FP64 endpoint](../fp64_sketch_endpoint_v1/RESULT.md), [negative modes](../negative_covariance_modes_v1/RESULT.md), [RQMC result](../../astra_phase2_reexamination_v1/current_first_layer_quadratic_rqmc_v1/RESULT_CLOSED.md).

Ordinary sampling is not categorically forbidden by the official documentation, but it is expensive at the required variance. A useful control variate must have a known expectation, affordable coefficients, and sufficiently small residual variance; a bank prediction is not automatically an exactly known control expectation. The archived polynomial-control study already accounted for substantial construction and per-sample work. Its unsupplied high-capture control remains the hard part. Likewise, two independent equal-error estimators averaged together halve unbiased uncorrelated error but double cost; above the scoring floor that alone gives no adjusted gain. Correlated errors make that case worse. Shared computation, negative error correlation, or distillation would be needed to change the economics. [Control-cost audit](../../astra_phase2_reexamination_v1/JOINT_DOUBLE_INSERTION_AND_INPUT_CHAOS_ADMISSION_2026_09_07_V1.md).

Official allowed-code documentation permits trained parameters but requires metered numerical execution; it does not allow numerical work to escape through compiled/FFI calls or multi-value packing for accounting advantage. The possible methods here must obey that model. Only the accessible starter-kit documentation was reviewed; the challenge-rules webpage did not expose its full text during this review. [Allowed code](https://github.com/AIcrowd/whest-starterkit/blob/main/docs/concepts/allowed-code.md).

**Decision sequence after the current fit.** Let the existing sole launch owners complete the fixed fit and, conditionally, native/downstream/release stages. If all gates pass, use standing approval to submit once and verify the complete official result. Preserve a failed result as failed. Then prioritize one new mechanism using the evidence the terminal native comparison actually supplies: useful-but-expensive information favors a compact student; useful moments harmed by autonomous feedback favor a genuinely coupled consumer/state design; lack of useful new information favors the small constructive joint-remainder test. A native discrepancy justifies trainer work only if a bounded diagnostic demonstrates it. Keep inexpensive, separately validated gains eligible for banking throughout.

Any next numerical study needs a fixed scientific question, source/version bindings, a complete cost case, a finite resource budget, and an explicit stopping rule before evaluation. Use one numerical process group, the existing memory/swap guards, and the 470 GB free-disk floor. No new bulk dataset is required by this note. Do not mistake reused research networks for independent holdouts, or promote an oracle coefficient. The current evidence supports several well-defined tests; it does **not yet support a validated path to approximately 1e-9**.
