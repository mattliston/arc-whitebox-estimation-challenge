## Fixed fit and objective

For each fold separately, fit the affine control on A (65 columns), and
the extended head on [A,Z] (130 columns). Target = ordinary1B final mean
minus parent served FP32 final prediction. No intercept outside those
columns, no nonlinear model or feature selection. Every network/neuron
receives its parent's complete cold multiplier; normalize weights to sum1.
Each column is divided by its fit-only weighted RMS floored1e-12. Fit the
unique ridge system X.T*w*X+1e-3 I. Penalize every coordinate, including the
constant hidden-direction column. No cross-fold preprocessing or tuning.
Decode only the16 fit targets when fitting each model. Write all five models
and held-network predictions before the separate scoring stage opens held
targets for evaluation. Across folds a network may be training for a different
model; the model serving that network never uses its target. This is CV, not
a globally label-blind protocol. Save exact fit/held rosters for every model.

Serve max(parent served final + design@unscaled_beta,0).astype(float32).
Original first15 rows remain unchanged. Verify the weighted ridge solver on
fixed small fixtures against independent augmented least squares, including
constant/zero/duplicate/signed columns. Verify the served head with actual
FNP against NumPy, repeat bytes/bill and zero-coefficient identity. On all
wide folds verify normal-equation residual<=1e-9 relative (absolute at zero)
and coefficients versus an independent Cholesky solve<=1e-8 relative.

Score each held network using its own measured complete cold parent bill.
Affine receives an additional2MF readout allowance; extended receives actual
measured helper cost plus2MF. The allowance is provisional pending full
native integration. Do not describe these as measured full candidate bills.
Compute per-net raw*max(.1,F/2^41), aggregate actual products, verify raw losses
with math.fsum. Advance ONLY if extended pooled adjusted/parent<=.99,
extended/affine<=.99, at least12/20 individual parent wins, at least4/5
parent-improving folds, and worst individual adjusted ratio<=1.20.

Failure closes this fixed transfer test; no alternate penalty, controls-only
deployment, coefficient shrinkage, feature change, layer or nonlinear rescue.
A pass licenses a separately specified all20 fit, unused-by-this-fit CHECK
and actual native integration, followed by panel/confirmation/client/contract
and package validation. This study alone can neither upload nor claim a
milestone. Qualified future uploads remain user-authorized.
