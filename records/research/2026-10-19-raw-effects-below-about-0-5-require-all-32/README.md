# Raw effects below about 0.5% require all 32 paired networks rather than two or seven

2026-10-19T07:08:49Z · lesson · informational · measured

The same seven ranks that found the join-sketch-lite bill cut cannot resolve raw changes of 0.1-0.5%: the half carrier shrink (-0.29% -> -0.01%), the dead-unit floor (-0.29% -> +0.02%) and the sketch column squeeze (+0.04% on two ranks -> -0.39%) all flipped on 32 ranks. Every value-changing arm is therefore judged on all 32 store ranks (28 minutes, two lanes) before it counts and the seven-rank judge is kept for byte-identical bill parts only. Binding and lazy-bind cuts transfer to the platform at about half, product-size cuts in full.

Recorded measurement:

    HALF 7-rank -0.283% -> 32-rank -0.007%; dead-unit floor 0.002 -0.29% -> +0.015%; kc squeeze 2-rank +0.04% -> 32-rank -0.390%; #337061 local bill -0.10% -> hosted util -0.053%; local 32-rank numbers conservative by ~0.06 points for this lineage
