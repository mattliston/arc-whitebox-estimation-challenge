# Cold-output work reduction: current-kernel source preflight

Phase 2 only. This source check follows the completed orthogonal cubic
merge failure and selects no new inference candidate. It does not tune
that merge, reopen its slice constructor or reuse its verification targets.

The older cold-output CP trace experiment approximated only cold scalar
third-cumulant reads, keeping active carriers and birth masks intact. On
the unlearned older frame, raw worsened 3.23645% and 0.39963%; the first
missed its <=3% gate. Its 14.17%/14.07% output-row push reduction was a
row-count proxy, never a complete bill. Thus it did not establish an
adjusted-score benefit on the current submitted Lazy estimator.

The current source gives a concrete additional cost obstacle. The LAST
_rule_depth admits depth6 for wide push only at exactly 1024 output rows
(and dimensions divisible by64). _packed_product_4 has the matching
1024-row contract. Other row counts use at most depth4. The immutable
weight-cache grants also require a 1024-row left operand at their four
explicit owner sites. Trimming all weight pushes would violate that
contract unless explicitly changed and requalified.

A narrower hypothetical change could keep the full weight/basis push
and young/newborn push, trimming only _slr_reconstruct's old-factor
products. The existing live masks are rounded to32, but that alone does
not retain the six-level kernel. The stdlib audit evaluates the unchanged
selector and its arithmetic formula on a declared illustrative shape grid;
it decodes no arrays and measures no current-network shape census.

For a 1024x384 times384x8128 old-factor reconstruction, the current rule
selects depth6 and estimates 3,420,852,050 arithmetic operations. Reducing
the output to896 rows selects depth4 and estimates 3,420,401,376: ratio
0.9998682568, despite removing12.5% of rows. At928 rows the ratio is
1.034940768; at960 it is1.070013279. At832/864 rows there are modeled
savings of about7.03%/3.52% for this product. Shapes2048/6144/16384 show
the same issue. These are conditional operation formulas, not complete
flopscope bills, runtime measurements, whole-card bounds or an assertion
that every row-pruning configuration loses.

Trace construction, fixed solves, gathers/scatters, scalar replacement,
copies, cache changes and the other original work remain additional.
In the current Lazy schedule, obtaining a post-join exact old-tensor
partial trace may also require work on deferred factors; an inexpensive
trace from pre-join stored columns would be a distinct approximation.
The final scalar branch would need a cold mask before its cubic read.
The original mean head still reads all scalar coordinates. No such
implementation, paid error/cost result or arbitrary-row six-level kernel
is supplied here. A simple row-count extrapolation is not a sound reason
to port the older approximation.
