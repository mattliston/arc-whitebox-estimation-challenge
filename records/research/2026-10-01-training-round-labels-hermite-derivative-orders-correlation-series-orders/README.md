# Training-round labels, Hermite derivative orders and correlation-series orders describe different quantities

2026-10-01T05:14Z · lesson · informational · derived

Historical labels such as R7 and R7s designate learned-training rounds rather than seventh-order correlation expansions. The current recurrent Gaussian pair map stops at correlation order three, its non-Gaussian bond expansion keeps orders zero and one, layer zero uses a separate Gaussian order-six map, and the terminal scalar output has no pair map. Seven stored Hermite derivative planes and similarly named arithmetic kernels do not change those truncation orders.
