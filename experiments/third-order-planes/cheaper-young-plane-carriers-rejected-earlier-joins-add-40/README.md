# Cheaper young-plane carriers rejected: earlier joins add 40-48% MSE; rank-256 reads add 131%

2026-10-10T20:48Z · experiment · rejected · measured

Joining young cohorts into the old tier earlier, or reading the young plane on the top eigenvectors of C, destroys accuracy: the young bank's exact content and the plane's fine structure carry it, and the moment-closure estimator is hypersensitive to any approximation of them.

Recorded measurement:

    joins every 2 layers from 4 +47.5% for -19% bill; every 3 from 5 +40.6%; cohorts 9-11 joined at 14 +3.6% for -2.9%; top-256 / 512 eigenvectors +131% / +10.5% (97.3 / 99.8% energy kept); plane's own rank-512 SVD +2.0%
