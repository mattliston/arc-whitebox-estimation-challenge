# Terminal response-head statistics: ten pair vectors, squared norms and clipping admit no linear factorisation

2026-09-08T10:27Z · finding · rejected · derived

The final learned response head (P head) consumes ten pair-summary vectors: pre-correlation mean, RMS, absolute mean and rho-alpha, K21 row and column sums and squared norms, and clipped post-covariance row mean and RMS. Linear sums factorize, but the squared norms need factor-index Grams and cross terms and the final covariance mixes both directed K21 entries with correlation polynomials before clipping, so no dominant exact product could be removed from the terminal contraction; only the dead final covariance assignment remained as a micro-cut.
