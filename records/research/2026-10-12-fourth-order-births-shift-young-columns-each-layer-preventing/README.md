# Fourth-order births shift young columns each layer, preventing a fixed pushed common-part basis

2026-10-12T03:39:22Z · finding · informational · measured

The planned native pair-split scheme (one basis built at layer 8 and pushed layer to layer, F carried) was void because F4C shifts the young cohorts' columns at the end of every layer for ages <= _F4C_AGE by a per-element quadratic form ((A o + B Delta) o + C Delta^2), so c = U F breaks for every cohort still in its window and only cohorts aged >= 3 evolve linearly. The PyTorch control confirmed it independently (full-rank pushed basis: c energy 0.99986 at L9 falling to 0.9995 at L14, 1.4% per layer off the base), and a v2 that projects each pair's in-place shift onto the pushed basis passed (energy 1.00000000, within 8.4e-5 of the base); the shifts carry 1.6-3.1e-4 of the c energy per layer. The realistic rank-32 extension arm read +1.44% on 4 networks before the pushed-basis line was dropped for a per-layer Nystrom basis.

    control v1: c energy 0.99986 at L9 -> 0.9995 at L14, 1.4% per layer off the base; v2: 1.00000000 energy, base within 8.4e-5 per layer; shifts 1.6-3.1e-4 of the c energy per layer; R32: final +1.44% (se 0.79, 1 of 4 lower), scheme c energy 0.9990-0.9992 vs oracle 0.9994-0.9998 at L9-14
