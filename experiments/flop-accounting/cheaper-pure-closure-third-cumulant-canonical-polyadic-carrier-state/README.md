# Cheaper pure-closure third-cumulant canonical-polyadic carrier state co-design: measured and rejected

2026-08-27 · experiment · rejected · measured

The pure-closure third-cumulant canonical-polyadic carrier (CP3) compression family is terminal at its measured scope. Blind age retirement and fixed original-column summaries failed first; the synthesized quotient nested-HT teacher screen then also failed. At a projected 551,371,243,519 FLOPs its rank-0 raw quotient and raw-control MSEs are 6.773519569e-8 and 6.668580156e-8, both more than twice the 3.060640051e-8 promotion gate.
