## Distributional propagation remains open only beyond the tested simplifications

The existing residual-preserving audit already distinguishes a Gaussian
factor reparameterization from nonlinear conditional propagation. A state
`m + U z + epsilon` with independent Gaussian z and constant full residual
covariance is exactly one Gaussian. Refitting that same form cannot acquire
new non-Gaussian information. Retaining nonlinear conditional means and
node-dependent full covariances is different, but a Gaussian reset discards
the within-component third cumulant. A Q-node mixture's mean span is at
most Q-1; this limitation must not be assigned to an arbitrary continuous
nonlinear latent function.

The adaptive positive GMM4 actually retained full component covariances and
executed. Its raw35/100 errors were 2.61823e-6 and 4.25983e-6, about .9040
and .9200 of its own Gaussian baseline, far above the bank. A persistent
fixed-input-latent quadrature is not that exact split/merge rule, and has
not been numerically closed by the GMM4 result. However, no new cheap
within-component skew supplier or adequately accurate finite rule was
identified here. The earlier Q4/Q8 factor-ownership subtotals, 294.205BF
and 586.263BF, omit maps and other work and are not complete measured bills.

The separate four-bin hidden-energy oracle remains formally
`INCONCLUSIVE_FIXED_ACQUISITION`: its mixture/bank cross-error ratio was
9.5922, but the bank noise/cross ratio .9369 violated its .10 qualification
limit. Its omitted-within-bin-third ratio 74.6179 is a diagnostic, not a
replacement gate. This audit does not turn that result into a statistically
resolved failure or expand its samples, bins or thresholds.
