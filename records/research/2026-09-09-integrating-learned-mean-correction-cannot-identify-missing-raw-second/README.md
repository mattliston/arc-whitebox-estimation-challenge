# Integrating a learned mean correction cannot identify the missing raw second-moment correction

2026-09-09 · finding · rejected · derived

The scalar identity relating the location derivative of the ReLU raw second moment to twice its mean is already represented in the Gaussian formulas. Integrating an arbitrary learned mean residual over location does not establish the true raw-second-moment error or its joint covariance correction.

Recorded measurement:

    d_mu E[ReLU(X)^2]=2 E[ReLU(X)] for a location shift;no numerical experiment or new score.
