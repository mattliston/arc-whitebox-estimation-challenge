## 2. The natural quadratic constructor changes its input moments

Take symmetric C positive definite, symmetric third tensor T and Z~N(0,C).
The proposed centered perturbative constructor is

    X_i = Z_i + (1/6) T_iab
          [(C^-1 Z)_a (C^-1 Z)_b - (C^-1)_ab].

Let A_i=(1/6)C^-1/2 T_i C^-1/2, using the symmetric square root. Wick
contractions give its EXACT moments

    Cov(X_i,X_j) = C_ij + 2 tr(A_i A_j),
    kappa3(X_i,X_j,X_k) = T_ijk + 8 tr(A_i A_j A_k).

The first third-order contribution is T by its permutation symmetry;
the cubic trace remains. Symmetry of the A matrices makes the trilinear
trace symmetric under all permutations. Thus the constructor matches T
only to first order and changes covariance at second order. Using its
exact higher moments with the UNCHANGED bank C,T is not a moment-preserving
law. Preserving both would need paid counterterms or a specified nonlinear
self-consistency solve, including numerical/PSD qualifications.

Even within quadratic-Gaussian laws, C and T do not determine fourth
moments. For centered scalar quadratic forms, cumulants of order r>=2
are 2^(r-1)*(r-1)! times the sum of eigenvalues to power r. Eigenvalues

    (1/2,-1/2),
    (1,-1,1,-1)/(2*sqrt(2))

both give variance1 and third cumulant0, but fourth cumulants6 and3.
These are exact distributional witnesses, NOT Phase2 performance bounds
or evidence that any particular chosen closure cannot be useful.
