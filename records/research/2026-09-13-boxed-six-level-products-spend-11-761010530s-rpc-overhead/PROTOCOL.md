overhead. Let K/O/R be the client top-level RPC backend/overhead/residual.
The actual server `_run_kernel` wraps a native FNP function, not just NumPy;
therefore K generally includes H. Report the signed unassigned compute gap
G=K-B-H, without clamping or guessing a cause. Reconcile
`wall = B + H + G + O + R`. O, named `rpc_reported_overhead_s`, is RPC dispatch
minus server function time. It includes client/server dispatch, serialization
and communication, not a separately measured network cause. **H is not a
subset of O**, and O-H is not an attribution formula.

This separates primitive categories such as matmul, QR, add, subtract, stack,
copyto and indexing. It does not provide per-layer, per-shape or per-dispatch
timing, nor a decomposition of wire/serialization/free overhead within O.
