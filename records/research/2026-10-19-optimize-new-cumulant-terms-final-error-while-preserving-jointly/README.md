# Optimize new cumulant terms for final error while preserving jointly fitted corrections

2026-10-19T22:26:00Z · lesson · informational · derived

The mean and pair formulas are truncated Gram-Charlier-type expansions whose dials were fitted end to end on the final MSE, so the k3 and k4 they consume are effective quantities that absorb the truncation and the compensation of every other term; truth-optimal scales (k3 1.1-1.5, k4 1.05) are not MSE-optimal and the k3 loop is razor-sharp. Truth probes are used only to find where structure exists; any new term is fitted on the final MSE (store or fresh-network finite differences) with the co-adapted dials free to move, never on cumulant truth.
