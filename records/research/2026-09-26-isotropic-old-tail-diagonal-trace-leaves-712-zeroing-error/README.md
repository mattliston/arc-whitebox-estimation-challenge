# Isotropic old-tail diagonal trace leaves 7–12% of zeroing error in six captured cases

2026-09-26 · finding · informational · measured

An unchanged-estimator capture measured old-diagonal reconstruction at layers 7, 10 and 14 on two training networks. An isotropic trace was useful locally, a mean-direction Hermite correction helped modestly, and a four-term fitted correction was unstable.

Recorded measurement:

    Six layer/network cells: isotropic trace leaves about 7–12% of positive-tail diagonal squared error of zeroing; capture 108.10 MB; all prediction bytes unchanged.
