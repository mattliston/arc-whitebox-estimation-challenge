# Bini <3,2,2> approximate-product defect is dense: correcting four edge terms costs 3.24 GF against a 0.26 GF margin

2026-09-08T09:01Z · finding · rejected · derived

Expanding the ten products of the Boyer-Dumas Bini <3,2,2> rank-10 circuit gives C_eps = AB + eps E1 + eps^2 E2 with explicit first-order defects in all six output blocks (E1 = [[-bg, bh], [(b-f)i-(d+e)g+(c+f)j, (a+d)g+(e-a)h-(b+c)j], [ei, -ej]]), so the error is neither diagonal nor antisymmetric and is not nulled by the cubic-diagonal reset, by paired polarization or by any same-ten-product linear correction. On the archived 1024x832x8192 push geometry the four edge-error correction products alone exceed the earlier approximate-multiplication gross margins, so the free-correction idea was rejected and no prototype or precision sweep admitted.

Recorded measurement:

    4*M3(344,416,4096) = 3,243,694,160 F and 4*M4(352,416,4096) = 3,016,033,328 F vs earlier APA gross subtotal margins 259,620,408 F and 828,214,920 F
