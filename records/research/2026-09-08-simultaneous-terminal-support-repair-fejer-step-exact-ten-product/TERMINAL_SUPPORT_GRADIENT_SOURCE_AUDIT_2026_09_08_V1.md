## 1. Constraints and fixed affine slice

For H>=0, m=E H, C=Cov(H), centered third tensor T, and any real w,
the weighted second-moment localizer gives, for m_i>0,

    chi_i(w) = m_i^2 (w.T C w) + m_i T(e_i,w,w) - (Cw)_i^2 >= 0.

The division-free expression does not justify dividing by a zero/negative
approximate m_i. For a true m_i=0, H_i=0 almost surely supplies additional
zero-moment constraints. Near-dead marginal defects must not be relabeled
as a warm joint-support witness.

Set W=[w_j] to the terminal weights, h_ij=max(-chi_i(w_j),0), and
A_ij=m_i h_ij. Let Sym average the three placements of its first leg;
Pi_d is the Frobenius-orthogonal projection removing all repeated-index
entries. The negative gradient of .5*sum h_ij^2 within the fixed-repeated
affine slice is

    N = sum_j Sym(a_j,w_j,w_j),        G = Pi_d N.

This presumes the fixed lower-moment and repeated-entry owners used by
the constraints. Additional support/masking restrictions require their own
projection; they are not silently part of Pi_d.

## 2. Projection, norm and terminal reader

Define the repeated plane of N (NOT that of the reference tensor):

    D = [2(A*W) W.T + (W*W) A.T]/3,   d=diag(D).

Here * denotes entrywise multiplication. Its all-distinct projection has
the cubic polynomial

    G[z^3] = sum_j (a_j.T z)(w_j.T z)^2
              - 3*(z*z).T D z + 2*sum_i d_i z_i^3.

With K=W.T W, U=W.T A and H=A.T A,

    ||G||_F^2 = <H,K*K>/3 + 2*<U*U.T,K>/3
                  - 3*||D||_F^2 + 2*||d||^2.

The norm difference can cancel badly; exact real arithmetic is not a
floating-point certificate. Negative rounded values cannot be clamped
and described as a proved Fejer step.

The pushed repeated plane of N is

    D_N' = [2*(U*K) K.T + (K*K) U.T]/3.

Let M=D W. The pushed repeated-sector countertensor has plane

    D_rep' = (W*W).T [M - 2*d[:,None]*W]
                + 2*(W*M).T W.

Thus DeltaK21=eta*(D_N'-D_rep') and Deltak3=diag(DeltaK21).
Five square products form K/U/H/D; two form D_N'; three form M/D_rep'.
This ten-product reader is O(n^3), not a dense third tensor or an appended
CP bank. It is distinct from changing the old single-ray rank/strength.

The same tensor must be contracted before any explicitly declared response
mask. Giving mixed terms and repeated counterterms different implicit
CP/star masks breaks the identity. Original-coordinate repeated preservation
does not imply repeated preservation after a generic W.

## 3. What the Fejer statement actually guarantees

For any feasible T_star in the SAME fixed-repeated-entry affine slice,

    <G,T_star-T> >= sum h_ij^2.

Consequently

    ||T+eta*G-T_star||^2
      <= ||T-T_star||^2 - 2*eta*||h||^2 + eta^2*||G||^2.

For nonzero G, eta=||h||^2/||G||^2 gives a decrease of at least
||h||^4/||G||^2. This is neither the exact projection onto all halfspaces
nor a guarantee that a single step satisfies them all. If G=0 while a
violation remains, the displayed step is undefined; no denominator repair
is authorized by the proof.

Truth is an eligible T_star only if its m/C/repeated owners coincide with
the ones fixed here. The current learned/approximate state does not provide
that certificate. Even with exact owners, Frobenius error decrease is NOT
a guarantee about a particular final ReLU/GNN consumer's error. The latter
depends on weighted directions, marginal and covariance changes, clipping,
and the nonlinear P head. A generic unequal directional weighting does not
inherit Euclidean Fejer monotonicity.

## 4. Acquisition, complete ownership and explicit price model

All-coordinate support acquisition is not the old four-coordinate query.
For a coherent physical post tensor with factors P and weights lambda,
its CP query matrix is

    Q_ij = T_CP(e_i,w_j,w_j)
         = [(P*lambda) ((W.T P)*(W.T P)).T]_ij.

Active/cold star and covariance-trace contributions, Cw, and the implicit
newborn factors are additional. Native rounded pp from the existing FP32
Winograd push cannot simply be called the exact W.T P of the declared
post14 tensor. One needs an error certificate or a separately paid coherent
shadow contraction. Reconstructing the implicit newborn must retain its
zero-diagonal base, selected coordinates, signs and stored precision.

For a straightforward full-width FP64 shadow design, R<=2*15*n. Two
rectangular products form W.T P and the query Q. Two square products form
the full physical star query, one forms the active-metric B W (cold Cw
rows are then restored separately), and ten form the gradient/reader above.
Using classical2*m*k*n arithmetic and FP64 rate2 gives the DOMINANT subtotal

    8*n^2*R + 52*n^3.

At n1024,R30720 this is313,532,612,608F. Adding the qualified mean parent
cost gives779,591,344,275.9286F BEFORE remaining assembly, reductions,
casts/copies, feasibility/rounding guards and initialization changes.
This mixes a measured mean parent with a full-width MODEL of additional
work; it is a planning cost estimate, not a measured complete program,
current shape census, or a universal lower/upper bound. Active dimensions,
newborn sharing or a different qualified precision could change it.
The full program is therefore roughly.78T plus unpriced overhead in this
specific design, with no certified runtime or RSS result. A smaller leading
