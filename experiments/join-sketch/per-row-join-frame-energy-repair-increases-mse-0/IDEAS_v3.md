## 3. Analysis recipe for the FD data (U(B), U(B +- d_i) at B0 and B1, 6 nets)
1. Per net n and dial i, in log units: a_i^n(B) = [ln U(B + d_i) - ln U(B - d_i)] / 2 (slope per FD step), b_i^n(B) = ln U(B + d_i) + ln U(B - d_i) - 2 ln U(B) (curvature per step^2). Pool: mean and se over the 6 nets; a slope is real if |mean| > 2.5 se and the sign holds on >= 5/6 nets (per-net sd of a +-20% dial move is 0.2-0.5% from the record; se ~0.1-0.2%).
2. Diagonal Newton at B1 with the profile fixed: t_i = -a_i(B1) / max(b_i(B1), b_min) in units of the FD step, clipped to |t_i| <= 1 (never extrapolate past the measured points; b_min = the median positive curvature of the set, to stop a flat dial from taking a huge step). Predicted gain of dial i alone: g_i = -a_i^2 / (2 b_i) (clipped consistently). Apply only dials with a real slope.
3. Interaction with the profile: D_i = a_i(B1) - a_i(B0) is the mixed derivative d^2U / (d d_i dc) x c_half. Substitutes (D_i has the opposite sign to a_i(B0), i.e. the slope relaxes) must not be double-counted: their Newton step at B1 already includes the profile; complements (D_i has the same sign as a_i(B0) or a slope appears from zero) are the 'move together' dials.
4. Unmeasured cross terms d_i x d_j: assume the Hessian is block-diagonal with two blocks, EARLY = {gsk, kc_early, iso_early, k21n_early, gk4_early} and LATE = {edge, iso_late, k31}; within a block the dials act on the same scalar (the early injection; the late compensation), so their effects are NOT additive: within a block take the SINGLE best dial at its Newton step, or scale every dial in the block by 1 / sqrt(k) for k dials moved (the coherent-sum rule for same-direction perturbations). Across blocks, add. Then damp the whole step by 0.5 (the record's dials all read 1:1 or worse when stacked at full size), and let the held-out read calibrate the damping: realized / predicted gain > 0.7 -> go to full step on the judge; < 0.3 -> cross terms dominate: run the pairwise FD of the two largest dials (4 settings) before any judge lane.
5. Held-out that decides: one setting = profile + the damped joint step, on fresh 7100011-14 with the base (8 predicts, one process): decisive if lower on >= 3/4 and the mean gain is within [0.5, 1.5] x the prediction; then the science copy on the 32 judge ranks vs science32_rel_v4a_m15 (the only read that counts, 22 min). Fire bar unchanged (-0.35% adjusted, held-out 25 negative).
