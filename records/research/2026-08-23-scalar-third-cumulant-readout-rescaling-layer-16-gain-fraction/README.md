# Scalar third-cumulant readout rescaling: layer-16 gain fraction below 0.004 versus 0.066 at layer 2

2026-08-23T19:36Z · finding · rejected · measured

A target-free reconstruction asked whether an optimal scalar multiple of the difference between two sealed predictions (Hermite order 4 to CP cap 1792, and CP cap 512 to cap 1792) could close the remaining error. At layer 2 the optimal scalar is 1.16 to 1.19 and recovers 6.6 to 12.4 percent of the error; at layer 16 the optimal scalars 1.41 and 1.64 recover under 0.4 percent. A one-dimensional CP-amplitude readout therefore has a small correctable residual at layer 2 and none at layer 16.
