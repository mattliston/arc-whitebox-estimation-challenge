# Finite multiplication catalog on the delayed-rotation estimator saves at most 15.19%/16.11% versus 21.39% needed

2026-09-20 · experiment · rejected · measured

The finite fast-matrix-multiplication family (six executable grouped Smirnov orientations plus the bank Winograd, extended by 36 rectangular and two rank-48 catalog entries) that had shown about 21% optimistic savings on the older roughly 490 BF predecessor was repriced on the exact 335-call censuses of the accepted lazy-rotation card. Even the extended catalog saves only 15.1940%/16.1147% of the bill on nets 35/100, short of the 21.392902% uniform saving that unchanged accuracy would need for 3e-9, so the standalone cost route fails its necessary gate; v1 and v2 of the reprice are preserved with metadata assertion failures.
