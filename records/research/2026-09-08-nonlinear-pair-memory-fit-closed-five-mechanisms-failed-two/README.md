# Nonlinear pair-memory fit closed: five mechanisms failed; two-channel latent transport costs 128.85 GF

2026-09-08T10:58Z · decision · rejected · measured

A review of the closed joint-edge, graph and memory designs showed that nonlinear pair memory with true joint supervision was already an executed mechanism: a full covariance-error observer improved final raw 2.99% (insufficient for its matrix cost), the joint-edge covariance decoder reached held-out covariance ratios .8945/.8911 and next-mean .9948/.9522, the endpoint-history decoder .7414/.7444 and .9933/.9855, the current memory model's plane 1.01099 with both head consumers worse, and the joint graph probe mean-residual ratio 1.000888. A congruence-transported K21 channel is latent without a three-leg decoder, and the transport of two full latent channels across 15 transitions would need about 22% raw improvement to break even under a uniform-cost yardstick (51% for 3e-9), so no further fit was launched.
