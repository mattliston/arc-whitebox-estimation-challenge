# Value-weighted Nyström row selection loses to the existing row rule

2026-09-25T19:08Z · experiment · rejected · measured

Weighting sampled rows by values worsened the mean squared error in every tested setting on 24 training networks. No variant was promoted.

Recorded measurement:

    - 2026-09-25 12:08 covhunter: VALUE-WEIGHTED JOIN SELECTION: FAIL at the TRAIN24 screen (RESULT sec 36; fable_covhunter_v1/r320_vsel.py; torch loopR3 card, 384/320/384, L10 penalty on TRAIN24 raw +0.964%). At the L10 join, Nystrom rows were ranked by energy x v^2 instead of energy alone. Change vs energy-only in pt raw: gate +0.17 [-0.30, +0.71]; next-layer row norm +0.12 [-0.24, +0.47]; k3-sensitivity loading +0.35 [-0.03, +0.76]; kink loading +0.28 [-0.15, +0.65]; weighted Nystrom (D_v A D_v, weighted coordinates) +0.35 [-0.04, +0.76]. All worse, so VALID40 and held-out were not run. Why: the value factors vary about 2x while the Gram diagonal spans orders of magnitude, so the value-ranked top 320 keep 297-313 of the energy top 320 rows; the few swapped rows carry no tail content and cost raw. Law (as in sec 24/35): the frame serves every downstream layer, energy is the right selection proxy, and the tail's error-aligned value is invisible to truth-free per-row scores. Machine free: nothing of mine running.
    - 2026-09-25 12:09 lead: VALUE-WEIGHTED JOIN SELECTION CLOSED (covhunter RESULT §36; TRAIN24 screen at 384/320/384): all five truth-free value scores are worse than energy-only selection (+0.12..+0.35 pt raw). Value factors vary about 2x across rows while the Gram diagonal spans orders of magnitude, so value-ranking swaps only 7-23 of 320 rows, and those swaps carry none of the tail content. Energy is the right proxy for a frame that serves every downstream layer.
