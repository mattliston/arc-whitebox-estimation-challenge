# Three-replica conditional mixed third source E[(M-m) x Sigma]: exact representation, no controlled variance

2026-09-08T07:19Z · finding · inconclusive · derived

For a fixed input projection A = U^T X with conditional mean M(A) and covariance Sigma(A), three forward paths sharing A with independent complementary inputs give the exact representation S = .5 (H1-H2)(H1-H2)^T and E[(H0-m) tensor S] = E[(M-m) tensor Sigma], i.e. the mixed third source without a conditional-covariance fit. It costs three complete prefix forwards per sample, omits E[third cumulant(H|A)] (four paths would be needed), and no controlled current-consumer variance exists after subtracting owned contributions; no estimator followed.
