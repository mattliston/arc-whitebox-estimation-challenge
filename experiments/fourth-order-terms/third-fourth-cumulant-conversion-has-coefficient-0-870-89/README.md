# Third-to-fourth cumulant conversion has coefficient 0.87–0.89 but adds 7.5% charged work

2026-10-01T22:00Z · experiment · rejected · measured

On network 35 at layers 5, 9 and 12 the fourth cumulant born from the third cumulant at one layer (a hub with one third-cumulant insertion and one covariance edge, computed from the card's bank) is physically right and small: added to the isotropic form it raises the explained variance of the true K31 at kink rows from 0.41 / 0.38 / 0.32 to 0.51 / 0.48 / 0.44 and explains 12-16% of the network-specific per-unit kurtosis. Its per-unit part needs one extra reduced read per layer (about +7.5% bill); the rest of the fourth-order content is older than one layer and costs at least as much as the third-order bank to carry.
