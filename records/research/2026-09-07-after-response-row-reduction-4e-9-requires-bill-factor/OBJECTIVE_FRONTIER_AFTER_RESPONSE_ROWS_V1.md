## Prospective evaluation discipline

For any new fixed proposal, record its source/state/head law, expected
effect and supporting evidence, COMPLETE cost estimate with uncertainty,
unresolved assumption, and cheapest decisive test BEFORE numerical work.
An exact closure theorem is not a prerequisite for testing an approximation;
neither is a suggestive tensor identity sufficient evidence to run one.

The comparison is the mean of the ACTUAL same-configuration products

    raw_i * max(.1, complete_F_i / 2^41),

not the product of group means, an isolated kernel saving, or an archived
raw paired with a newer implementation's bill. A raw increase is acceptable
when the actual adjusted objective improves. A raw-accuracy proposal with
added cost must earn that cost, including all state consumers and induced
changes in the rest of the card.

Retain small gains that pass the declared independent public8 AND virgin6
comparison and the unchanged numerical/resource/release gates. They may
be combined only with explicit state/head compatibility and fresh joint
validation; percentage gains are not additive. Keep milestone submission
policy: the response component is preserved and packaged, NOT uploaded.
