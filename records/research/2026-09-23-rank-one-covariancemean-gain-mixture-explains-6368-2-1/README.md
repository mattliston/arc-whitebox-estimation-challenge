# Rank-one covariance–mean gain mixture explains 63–68% of the (2,1)-plane error

2026-09-23T01:10Z · finding · informational · derived

On the #331824 card at layers 12-14 the K21 plane error against 8M truth is 63-68% rank-1 with left factor along the standard deviation and right factor along the mean; one scalar kappa times Sym(C, mu) explains 56-64% of its energy. For a common gain mixture the third cumulant contains (radial/6) Sym(C x mean), but the card's b recursion adds only the kink part of each radial increment, so b stalls at about a third of radial/6 and the star carries the rest approximately, producing the rank-1 error.

Recorded measurement:

    63-68% of error energy rank-1; left ~ sd (cos .92-.95), right ~ mu (cos .955-.965); kappa_l 4.8-5.6e-3 explains 56-64% (rel err .61-.64 -> .39-.40); card b aligned (cos .91) but ~2.6x too small; b = 0.32-0.41 x radial/6; k4 per unit ~ a v^2 + 0.7 k3^2/v (R^2 0.71-0.89 vs 0.58-0.76 isotropic)
