## Theorem 1: exact A/B projection split

Let F4 be the population sigma-algebra generated by the admissible exact state through fourth order and Finf the full state. Define

    m_inf = E[Y | Finf],
    m_4   = E[Y | F4].

For any fourth-order closure g4 in L2(F4), orthogonality of conditional projections gives

    R(g4) - R(m_inf)
      = ||m_4 - g4||_2^2 + ||m_inf - m_4||_2^2
      = A + B.

A is functional incompleteness: a wrong or incomplete function of information already present through order four. B is true hidden information unavailable from that state.

If the implemented formula class is V4, then

    A = ||m_4 - P_V4 m_4||^2 + ||P_V4 m_4 - g4||^2,

which separates representation error from calibration error.

With nested cumulant filtrations F4 subset F5 subset F6 subset Finf and m_r = E[Y | Fr],

    B = ||m_5-m_4||^2 + ||m_6-m_5||^2 + ||m_inf-m_6||^2.

These are the correct nonnegative definitions of genuine k5, k6, and tail value. Products such as K3^2 are F4-measurable and cannot honestly be assigned to B.

Nonidentifiability correction: the observed total 2.9396e-9 rest alone gives no information about its A/B split. For every theta in [0,T], the construction

    m_inf = g4 + sqrt(theta) u(F4) + sqrt(T-theta) Z,

with unit F4-measurable u and independent unit Z, has the same fourth-order state and total T but A=theta and B=T-theta.

## Checkpoint 5: minimal dynamic realization of the value-projected state

### Three ranks that must not be conflated

There are three different notions of rank:

1. the terminal readout rank (one for the fixed scalar endpoint);
2. the finite-horizon observable/Hankel rank of the transported hidden word;
3. the effective Kraus/provider rank of the local completely-positive bridge.

A scalar endpoint makes only the first rank equal to one.  It does not imply a
one-state realization or a one-Kraus implementation.

Let \(\mathcal H_l\) be the reachable, endpoint-observable residual quartic
quotient, \(T_l\) its frozen triangular transport, and \(c\in\mathcal H_L^*\)
the endpoint.  The smallest endpoint-observable module is defined backwards by
\[
 \mathcal U_L=\operatorname{span}\{c\},\qquad
 \mathcal U_l=\operatorname{span}\bigl\{T_{l,a}^*v:
 v\in\mathcal U_{l+1},\ a\text{ a reset/Kraus branch}\bigr\}
 \quad\bmod\mathcal N_l,
\]
where \(\mathcal N_l\) is the future endpoint-null subspace.  Its dimension
\(k_l=\dim\mathcal U_l\) is the time-varying observability rank, equivalently the
rank of the finite-horizon cut Hankel operator.  Exact \(k_l=1\) requires every
branch adjoint to preserve the same line modulo \(\mathcal N_l\).  For a CP map
this is a rank-one intertwining quotient, not merely a scalar terminal.

### What is exact for the currently stated triangular D14+D10 schedule

The currently auditable D10 definition is triangular.  Its baseline masks are
frozen, the quartic correction never feeds back into the D14 primal frame, and
the reverse mask obeys a single deterministic adjoint orbit.  Abstractly,
\[
 x_{l+1}=L_l(S_l)x_l+J_lh_l,qquad y=c^\top x_L,
\]
so with \(b_l=L_l^*b_{l+1}\),
\[
 y=\sum_l u_l,qquad u_l=\langle J_l^*b_{l+1},h_l\rangle .
\]
For this fixed endpoint and a **chosen lawful provider word**, one reverse mask
and one scalar value contraction per layer are therefore exact:
\(k_{\rm value}=1\).  The scalar means and variances add no dense GEMM if their
local contractions are fused into the existing reverse read.

This statement is conditional on the triangular architecture.  D14 presently
names the fourteen-transition signed-H3/frame schedule, but its actual local
nonlinear reset coefficients have not yet been derived.  A self-consistent
reset that feeds \(O\circ O\) or quartic state back into the even/primal frame

the scalar triangular Bellman decomposition.  Its exact state dimension is the
Hankel rank above.  With late word space
\(U_l=\operatorname{span}\{a_l,b_l\}\), the lifted H4 support

5, but earlier perpendicular births become structural value tail; they cannot

### No autonomous propagation from a generic order-at-most-four moment law

### 13.6 Closed scalar gradient/Hessian receipt

The exact-kernel jet does not require numerical differentiation.  Write

\[
\kappa(a,b,\rho)=\Psi(a,b,\rho)-\nu(a)\nu(b),
\qquad
A={a-\rho b\over s},\quad B={b-\rho a\over s}.
\]

Price's theorem and differentiation of truncated Gaussian moments give

\[
\boxed{
\kappa_\rho=\Phi_2(a,b;\rho),
\qquad
\kappa_{\rho\rho}=\varphi_2(a,b;\rho).
}
\]

The threshold gradients are

\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
\kappa_a={}&b\Phi_2(a,b;\rho)
+\varphi(b)\Phi(A)
+\rho\varphi(a)\Phi(B)-\Phi(a)\nu(b),\\
\kappa_b={}&a\Phi_2(a,b;\rho)
+\varphi(a)\Phi(B)
+\rho\varphi(b)\Phi(A)-\Phi(b)\nu(a).
\end{aligned}}
\]

The remaining independent Hessian entries are

\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
\kappa_{a\rho}&=\varphi(a)\Phi(B),
&\kappa_{b\rho}&=\varphi(b)\Phi(A),\\
\kappa_{ab}&=\Phi_2(a,b;\rho)-\Phi(a)\Phi(b),\\
\kappa_{aa}&=\varphi(a)
\left[s\varphi(B)+(b-\rho a)\Phi(B)-\nu(b)\right],\\
\kappa_{bb}&=\varphi(b)
\left[s\varphi(A)+(a-\rho b)\Phi(A)-\nu(a)\right].
\end{aligned}}
\]

Multiplying by \(\sigma_i\sigma_j\), subtracting the explicit Q6 or Q8
polynomial derivatives, and applying

\[
\dot a_i={\dot m_i-a_i\dot\sigma_i\over\sigma_i},
\qquad
\dot\rho_{ij}
={\dot C_{ij}\over\sigma_i\sigma_j}
-\rho_{ij}\left({\dot\sigma_i\over\sigma_i}
+{\dot\sigma_j\over\sigma_j}\right)
\]

gives the complete value/JVP/directional-Hessian interface using one
bivariate CDF and elementary scalar functions per pair.  It also yields the

\[

}
\]

because the centered covariance vanishes at \(\rho=0\).  This is an
