September6,2026 PDT (clock checked September7,04:14UTC). Phase2 only.

## Verdict

Three factor arrays plus one scalar coefficient per birth can retain the
original Gaussian signed pair INCLUDING its repeated-coordinate cubic
terms, while adding the exact linear heavy cross term. The scalar's cube
uses an already-carried factor and fuses into an existing plane product.
Thus four independently pushed CP atoms are not required by this source.

This is a potentially cheaper representation of a previously examined
two-edge physical source, not newly discovered raw information. The full
signed24-term source already failed a qualified recursive local-value gate.
The exact mixed compiler described here was not found as an executed arm,
but that distinction supplies neither a raw forecast nor reopening authority.
It must not be sold as a new physics success from the older .3927 source
ratio. Complete deployed cost and numerical fidelity are not established.

## 1. Authoritative birth algebra, including the persistent gauge

In the LAST active _predict_binary of the pinned official bank, a selected
birth hub a has zero-diagonal column B_i=g_i*C_ia and coordinate vector e_a.
Before rounding, its two CP atoms and common signed weight are

    pplus=B+t*e, pminus=B+s*e, weight=b/(t-s),
    b=d_a*(1+radial/3), u=t+s,
    w=t^2+t*s+s^2=(3*u^2+(t-s)^2)/4.

Here b is the scalar BIRTH coefficient, not the bank's covariance-trace
vector with the same code variable name elsewhere. Writing a vector as
its linear form when evaluating a polynomial, the COMPLETE pair is

    weight*(pplus^3-pminus^3)
       = 3*b*e*B^2 + 3*b*u*e^2*B + b*w*e^3.

Only the first term contributes all-distinct entries at birth. Moving the
other two terms into the current coordinate star preserves that instant's
tensor/repeated plane but not the bank's later recursion: old CP atoms
retain their gated all-distinct descendants, whereas the old coordinate
star is consumed through its pushed plane and then reset. That partition
is part of the current implementation, not an interchangeable gauge.

Let F already include the source's hub coefficient. To add exactly
6*e*B*F while retaining the original pair terms, set

    E=e,
    H=(b/2)*B + F + (b*u/2)*E,
    c=b*w,
    T_new(z)=6*(E dot z)*(B dot z)*(H dot z)+c*(E dot z)^3.

Expansion gives the original pair plus6*e*B*F exactly. Carry E,B,H by the
same affine maps and rowwise gates; c remains a birth coefficient. The
identity then persists in REAL arithmetic under those linear operations.
There is no new c-factor push.

The cE^3 term is generically necessary for this simple product form.
The quadratic P/e in coordinates(B,F,e) has matrix

    [[3*b, 3, 3*b*u/2], [3, 0, 0], [3*b*u/2, 0, b*w]],

whose determinant is -9*b*w. A single product of two linear forms has
quadratic rank at most2. This is a scoped algebraic obstruction to omitting
the scalar cube, not a global lower bound on tensor algorithms or shared
decompositions across different birth hubs.

## 2. Source signs, radial factors and exact readout

The eighteen non-Gaussian heavy terms in exact_heavy_source_slices_v1.py
use zero-diagonal B,D and

    D_ia = .5*g_i*K21_ai*eta_a + .5*d_i*K21_ia
           +.25*d_i*K22_ia*eta_a,
    F_ia = d_a*D_ia.

The six Gaussian doubled-edge terms used by the existing complete24-term
source add .5*e_a*d_i*C_ia^2 to F, where e_a is the Gaussian third mean
derivative (not the coordinate vector above). Their stored implementation
uses eta=-aux['alpha']/aux['sigma'] and e_a=d_a*eta_a. These formulae use
the specified actual pre-state, not a symmetrized or PSD-repaired substitute.

The older implementation writes Epath=D/(1+radial/3) and modifies
B*B.T into B*B.T+B*Epath.T+Epath*B.T. Since b=d_a*(1+radial/3), its
cross term is6*d_a*e*B*D, not6*b*e*B*D. The mixed formulation F=d_a*D
avoids dividing by1+radial/3 and therefore needs no new sign/positivity
premise. Negative coefficients are formal cumulant weights; no covariance
or mixture-positivity interpretation is claimed. Parent safeguards and
its actual t,s construction cannot otherwise be silently changed.

For columns E,B,H and coefficient vector c, the exact repeated plane is

    K21 = 2*(E*B) @ H.T + 2*(E*H) @ B.T
          + (2*B*H + E*E*c[None,:]) @ E.T.

The current scalar diagonal, including every cold unit, is

    k3_i = sum_a [6*E_ia*B_ia*H_ia + c_a*E_ia^3].

The three plane products may be one concatenated product with inner
width3R, or three R-width products. That changes dispatch/rounding, not
the classical bilinear count. The cE^3 read is included in the third
left operand, not a fourth GEMM.

At birth, zero-diagonal B,F make the added raw heavy plane exactly2*B*F
and its diagonal zero. The existing current-plane star rebasing must own
this change, the complete original CP plane, inherited content, and trace
content consistently. A literal accumulated history of the DE-REPEATED
source additionally transports its coordinate counterterms. That is not
automatically identical to adding a raw birth then applying the bank's
usual CP/star reset; the older source opportunity and a deployed recursive
law must not be conflated.

## 4. Full operation inventory required for any future compiler

Let n=1024, p be the previous active width, a the current active width,
R the number of retained birth hubs and r the youngest cohort's hubs.
Let M(m,k,n) mean the actual chosen kernel bill, including its padding.
The source-blind bank tapes can price a projection only; modified feedback
can change future memberships and counts.

1. **Affine factors and youngest sharing.** Old pairs pay
   M(n,p,2*(R-r))+M(n,p,r) plus first-push diagonal additions. Mixed
   factors pay M(n,p,3*(R-r))+2*M(n,p,r) plus additions: youngest E is
   directly W[selected,:].T, while B and F/H require two products.
   Subsequent E,B,H must ALL be pushed. The bank's already-shared first
   pair push is not a new saving. Column gates, masks, casts, concatenation,
   cohort metadata and full n output rows are required.
2. **Pre planes and full scalar reads.** Price all three a-by-R-by-a
   products, their left/right assembly, cE^2 term, and accumulation; or
   the exact concatenated3R kernel. Compute scalar diagonals from ALL n
   rows separately and restore them into the active plane. At final15,
   omit only the dense plane, not any factor push or scalar read.
3. **Source and newborn construction.** Form D and F from actual C,
   K21, K22 and local derivatives at every selected source layer. This
   is O(n^2) arithmetic but includes all multiplications/divisions,
   zero-diagonal operations, birth selection/gather, b,u,t,s,c, the H
   diagonal shift, active masks, casts and allocation. No source-specific
   covariance push, truth moment, eigensolve or hidden preprocessing is
   assumed free. Keeping every designated source is necessary for a pure
   representation comparison; no cheaper age/source subset is implied.
4. **Post-plane and star ownership.** Retain the original old-star push,
   its cold diagonals, and covariance-trace push/read. Inherit the complete
   mixed CP plane with the correct g_i^2*g_j factors. Add the actual new
   mixed birth plane; restore the original target minus mixed CP minus
   trace using the existing star convention. The bank's cached birth
   formula uses beta, while the pair polynomial uses b*u; clipping/safe-
   birth branches and stored-FP32 coefficients prevent silently treating
   these as the same numerical object. An exact compiler must certify its
   own as-executed bookkeeping and null-source parity.
5. **Feedback and every consumer.** Use the same full Gaussian/cubic pair
   maps, mean correction, covariance recentering, K4/radial reset, P0..14
   features/recurrent messages and scalar-final Q15. Changed K21 feeds
   all these states and future births. Holding them frozen is an
   information diagnostic, not a complete new estimator cost or result.
6. **Lifetime and runtime.** Charge factor ingress, copy/packing arenas,
   live/cold gathers, saved c vectors, mixed read workspaces, first-context
   initialization, and final output transformation. The mixed resident
   factor count is3R versus the old2R, with no fourth factor for c; this
   does not certify peak RSS. Rectangular D4/tile8192 bills and actual
   float32 rounding must be qualified, not inferred from classical counts.

Leading general push/read work is approximately1.5 times the old CP
block, not1.5 times the whole card and not automatically a .65T total.
The old current cost receipt places CP pushes/reads near392.9B of a
490.6B card. A naive1.5-times projection alone is therefore already near
.687T BEFORE genuinely new source/assembly charges, with sharing and
kernel choices requiring explicit evaluation. This is a planning
yardstick, not a complete upper bound or a measured deployment bill.

For ANY later authorization, advancement must use actual per-net final
raw MSE times max(.1, COMPLETE F/2^41), followed by independent panel and
runtime/numerical gates. Neither the source-only .3927 ratio nor a smaller
CP representation predicts that product. No raw magnitude is forecast.

## 5. Numerical and preservation boundary

The formula in section1 is exact before finite-precision reassociation.
Actual bank pplus/pminus and signed weights are stored FP32, and its
youngest shared product has its own rounding order. Reconstructing b,u,w
from ideal derivatives instead of the actual stored pair would not prove
parity. Even reconstruction from actual stored coefficients does not make
later three-factor pushes/gates commute with the old two rounded pushes.
No such numerical comparison was run in this audit.

This note preserves a useful compiler identity. It does not reopen the
closed signed source, positive pilot, q4 bridge, late full-query schedule,
or pair-jet screen; does not claim global impossibility; and does not
create a candidate, numerical exception, fit or test protocol.
